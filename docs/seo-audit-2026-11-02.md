# SEO audit: blta.sk and slta.sk

Date: 2 November 2026. Sites: blta.sk (Bratislava league) and slta.sk (national page plus the cities under it: /kosice, /trnava, /banska-bystrica).
Goal assumed: more organic visitors who become registered players (league sign-up), plus visibility for the court directory and the results/rankings pages.

## 0. How this was done, and what it does not cover

- **Live pages:** I fetched the raw HTML (what a crawler gets before any script runs) of about 20 public URLs on both sites, plus robots.txt, three sitemaps and about 25 status-code probes for legacy, trailing-slash, wrong-case and non-existent addresses. This is more than the 10-page sample the audit method normally allows, so treat the sample as "by template", not a full crawl.
- **Source code:** `src/seo.js`, the page router in `server.js`, the static file set, and the page templates.
- **Not available to me:** Search Console data, Core Web Vitals field data, index coverage, backlinks, rankings. My search tool cannot run `site:` queries, so I cannot say how many pages Google has indexed. Everything that depends on those is marked **Needs verification**, with the exact report to open.
- **Text counts:** character counts quoted below were taken from the raw HTML including inline scripts, so they are upper bounds for visible text. The one page I re-measured without scripts, slta.sk `/`, has about 410 words.
- **Evidence labels:** **Observed** = seen in fetched HTML, headers or code. **Needs verification** = depends on Google's rendering, index or field data.
- No promises about rankings or traffic. Each item names the mechanism and the metric to watch.

## 1. Executive summary

The technical foundation is better than most league sites: server-rendered titles, descriptions, canonicals, hreflang (sk/en/x-default), per-page noindex control in Backend > SEO, localized clean URLs, a sitemap with language alternates (392 URLs on blta.sk), JSON-LD on every template, real 404s for unknown paths, and Brotli compression. That is not the problem.

The ranking ceiling comes from five things:

1. **The real content is drawn by JavaScript from `/api/`, and `/api/` is blocked in robots.txt.** Google may see "Načítavam…" where the tables, rankings, player stats and match lists should be. (Highest impact; verify first.)
2. **Very little crawlable text.** blta.sk pages carry roughly 1,400 to 3,000 characters of raw text, most of it menu and footer. Only the blog articles (about 1,000 words) and slta.sk's home page (about 410 words of visible text) have real copy. There is almost nothing for Google to match to queries like "amatérska tenisová liga Bratislava".
3. **Thin editorial layer.** The blog has 4 articles in the sitemap. There are no guide, level or "how to join" pages, and no city landing pages for SLTA beyond Košice.
4. **Entity pages that do not exist return HTTP 200** (`/player/x`, `/pavuk/99999`, `/sezona/nope`) with no canonical and no robots tag. Old WordPress addresses such as `/project/autumn-finals-series-2026/` return 404 with no redirect.
5. **SLTA city copy is generated by find-and-replace**, which produces wrong Slovak ("v Košice", "Košice Ligy Tenisových Amatérov"), a typo ("Bytsrica") and a Košice URL called `/kosice/tenisove-kurty-v-bratislave`.

If you do only three things: fix item 1, add real text to the home pages and key templates, and start publishing content (section 6).

## 3. Findings, ranked

Impact: **High** = keeps goal pages from being crawled, rendered or indexed, or breaks a whole template. **Medium** = weakens how pages are understood or chosen. **Low** = polish.

### High

**H1. Main content is client-rendered and `/api/` is disallowed.** *Needs verification (code and robots are Observed).*
- Observed: robots.txt has `Disallow: /api/` (`src/seo.js:987`; on slta.sk also `/kosice/api/` etc., `src/seo.js:962`). The raw HTML of `/player/sloboda` has `<h1>Načítavam…</h1>`, `/pavuk/5` has `<h1>Loading…</h1>` with 586 characters of text in total, and `/zapasy`, the home pages and `/kosice/hraci` hold 1,200 to 3,000 characters including navigation and footer. Page files fill `#home-root`, `#sv-root`, `#court-root` from scripts.
- Why it matters: Google's renderer obeys robots.txt for the requests a page makes. If the data comes from `/api/...`, the rendered page can be empty even though the script runs. Bing, social previews and AI crawlers often do not render scripts at all.
- Check: Search Console > URL Inspection > a player, a season and `/zapasy` > *View crawled page* > HTML and Screenshot, and *More info > Page resources* for blocked `/api/` requests.
- Fix, best first:
  1. Render the key content on the server, as already done for the head and the intro heading (`PAGE_SSR` marker): the standings tables and rankings as real `<table>`s, a player's name, category, CourtIQ and W-L record as text, the latest results and next fixtures as links, a court's address and opening hours. The script can replace it after load.
  2. Quick interim step: allow the public read-only endpoints (`Allow: /api/...` for the exact GET paths the pages use) so rendering works while SSR is built. Keep admin and write endpoints blocked.
- Metric: URL Inspection shows the real content; Page indexing report: fewer "Crawled - currently not indexed" among player, bracket and season pages.

**H2. Pages for things that do not exist return 200.** *Observed.*
- `/player/does-not-exist`, `/pavuk/99999`, `/sezona/nope` return 200, the generic title ("BLTA — Hráč"), and no canonical or robots tag. Cause: `server.js:525`, `if (!entity) return hit.key === 'article' ? pageNotFound(res) : res.sendFile(...)`; only articles get a real 404.
- Why it matters: deleted or mistyped player, bracket and season URLs become soft 404s or duplicate shells, and wasted crawl.
- Fix: return `pageNotFound(res)` for every entity template when the lookup finds nothing (players, seasons, tournaments, brackets, courts, clubs), keeping the existing redirect for other cities' entities.

**H3. The old WordPress addresses do not redirect.** *Observed for the example; needs your list.*
- `/project/autumn-finals-series-2026/` returns 404. The source comments name old blta.sk addresses (`/project/…`, `/hraci/`, `/harmonogram/`, `/vitazi/`, `/tenisove-kurty-v-bratislave/`, `old.blta.sk/blog/`). The ones that kept their name still work; the `/project/<season>` ones do not.
- Fix: from Search Console (Performance > Pages, filtered to dates before the migration) or a WordPress export, list every old URL that had clicks or links, and add one 301 per URL to its new page. Redirect `/project/<slug>/` to `/sezona/<slug>`. Add a catch-all 301 only where the destination is the true equivalent.
- Metric: Page indexing > "Not found (404)" falls; clicks to the new URLs recover.

**H4. Home pages have almost no indexable text, and the H1 says nothing.** *Observed.*
- blta.sk `/`: H1 is "Prehľad ligy" ("League overview") and about 1,400 characters of text in the raw HTML, mostly navigation. The same H1 is on `/kosice/`, `/trnava/`, `/banska-bystrica/`, and on `/en` the H1 is still Slovak. The text that explains what BLTA is sits behind a button ("O nás") and loads by script.
- Fix: put a visible block of 300 to 600 words on the home page of each site: what the league is, who it is for, the three categories (Novice, Next Gen, Elite) and how a player's level is decided, how to join, season dates, price, and links to the next steps. Use an H1 that contains the topic, for example "BLTA: amatérska tenisová liga v Bratislave". slta.sk's home page already moves in this direction (about 410 words: offer, "Ako to funguje", questions and answers, partners); use it as the starting point for blta.sk and the city homes, and make it longer and more specific to the city and its levels.

**H5. Content gap: nearly all search demand is unserved.** See section 6.

### Medium

**M1. English pages serve Slovak body text.** *Observed.* `/en` has `<h1>Prehľad ligy</h1>`, and slta.sk `/en` has Slovak H1 and H2s; the translation happens in the browser. The English article page has an English title but a Slovak description (`Article.description` of `/en/rozhovor-s-tomasom-podhornym-hracom-blta`). Several English URLs keep Slovak slugs (`/en/rozhovor-…`, `/en/nove-tricka-pre-blta`). Googlebot may or may not render the translation, and non-rendering crawlers see the Slovak text under an `en` hreflang. Fix: render the language of the URL on the server (the intro heading and the main text), translate the article descriptions and slugs, and noindex English URLs that are not truly translated until they are.

**M2. SLTA city copy is wrong and some URLs name Bratislava.** *Observed in code and sitemap.*
- `src/seo.js:441-442` swaps "BLTA" for the brand and `Bratislav\w*` for the city name in the Slovak defaults, which gives "komunita rekreačných a amatérskych tenistov v Košice", "Košice Ligy Tenisových Amatérov", "Trnava Ligy Tenisových Amatérov". The Banská Bystrica description reads "Banská Bytsrica" (typed by an admin).
- `/kosice/sitemap.xml` lists `/kosice/tenisove-kurty-v-bratislave`: the page-address slugs are shared by every city (a comment in `seo.js` says so), so the courts list of Košice has "Bratislava" in its address.
- Fix: store the locative ("v Košiciach"), genitive and adjective forms per city and use them in the templates, and give the courts slug a neutral name for cities (`/kosice/tenisove-kurty`), with a 301 from the old one. Fix the typo in Backend > SEO.
- Also: titles on the Košice home end with a full stop ("…pre Košice a okolie.").

**M3. Titles and descriptions are too long or too short.** *Observed.*
| Page | Title length | Description length |
|---|---|---|
| blta.sk `/` | 70 | 175 |
| `/zapasy` | 53 | 134 |
| `/sezona/winter-opening-series-2027` | 73 | 193 |
| `/tenisove-kurty/ntc-bratislava` | 92 | 175 |
| article "Rozhovor s Tomášom Podhorným" | 89 | 242 |
| `/player/sloboda` | 68 | 92 |
| `/pavuk/5` | 87 | 88 |
- Cause: every page gets the 47-character suffix ` - BLTA - Bratislavská Liga Tenisových Amatérov` (`src/seo.js:13`). Search results cut titles at about 60 characters, so the page-specific part is often what gets lost at the end, and the suffix repeats on every result.
- Fix: suffix ` | BLTA` (7 characters), put the keyword first, aim for 50 to 60 characters. Descriptions: 120 to 160 characters, with a reason to click. Examples:
  - Courts: "Tenisové kurty v Bratislave: adresy, povrchy a rezervácia | BLTA"
  - Player: "Róbert Sloboda: tenisový hráč BLTA, štatistiky a výsledky" with the real category and record in the description.
  - Bracket: include the series and category: "Pavúk ELITE, Summer Rally Series 2026: výsledky a postup | BLTA".

**M4. Match share pages have no robots or canonical tag, and an English-only description.** *Observed in code.* `server.js:715` builds `/match/<token>` meta from a template with no `robots`, `canonical` or `description`; the Open Graph description is English ("Final score: …", "Live now") on a Slovak site. These pages are not in the sitemap, but they are shared publicly (WhatsApp, the blog). Decide deliberately: either `noindex, follow` for planned and live matches, and an indexable finished-result page (with a `SportsEvent` result, canonical, Slovak text), or noindex all. A finished match page can rank for "<player> tenis výsledky", but it is also private data about people, so this is a policy choice for you.

**M5. Structured data is minimal where it could earn more.** *Observed.*
- `SportsOrganization` (home) has no `sameAs`, `address`, `email` or `contactPoint`, although the footer has Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp, the address (Bárdošova 2939/23, Bratislava) and `info@blta.sk`. slta.sk's home has only `BreadcrumbList`: no `Organization` or `WebSite` for SLTA at all.
- `Article`: `publisher` has no `logo`; `author` is the organization, not a person.
- `SportsEvent` (seasons): `location` is only "Bratislava", with no venue or street address, and no `offers` (entry fee), so it does not meet what Google's event result needs. Courts have no `openingHours`.
- Fix: add the missing properties from the same fields the visible page uses; add a venue per event (the courts it is played on), `offers` from the entry fee, `openingHours` for courts. Note: FAQ rich results are limited to government and health sites, so the FAQ on slta.sk will not get a rich result, but keep it as text.

**M6. Static assets are not cached and the page loads a lot before it can show content.** *Observed; real impact Needs verification.*
- Live headers: `/css/style.css` and `/js/common.js` are served with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=0`. The server already gives `max-age=31536000, immutable` to files requested with `?v=` (`server.js:556`), but the HTML references them without it.
- Sizes (source): `style.css` 400 KB, `common.js` 239 KB, `i18n.js` 176 KB (both languages in one file), all unminified; nine scripts on the home page, the socket.io client on every page, flags loaded from `flagcdn.com` without width and height, no preload for the web font.
- Fix: version the asset URLs (`?v=<hash>`), minify CSS and JS, split `i18n.js` by language, load socket.io only on live pages, give flag images dimensions, preload the font, give the first image `fetchpriority`.
- Check: Search Console > Core Web Vitals (mobile, field data) and PageSpeed Insights for `/`, `/zapasy`, a season page. Lab scores alone are not a ranking factor.

**M7. The sitemap says nothing about freshness.** *Observed.* Of 392 URLs only blog posts and brackets have `<lastmod>`; seasons, players, courts and pages do not, and slta.sk's four URLs have none. Set `lastmod` from the real last change (a season's last result, a player's last match) and nothing else; Google ignores it if it is not accurate.

### Low

- **L1.** `twitter:card` is always `summary` (`src/seo.js:830`); use `summary_large_image` with a 1200×630 image for pages that have one. Many English pages fall back to the square logo as the preview image (`/en`, slta.sk `/en`, tournaments).
- **L2.** Trailing-slash and non-slash versions both answer 200 (`/zapasy/` and `/zapasy`, canonical is set). A 301 to one form is cleaner. On slta.sk the city homes canonicalize with a slash (`/kosice/`), the inner pages without; make that rule consistent in links, sitemap and canonical.
- **L3.** `robots.txt` blocks `/novy-zapas` and `/en/new-match` while the page also carries `noindex`; robots blocking stops Google from reading the noindex. Harmless for private pages, but keep one mechanism.
- **L4.** The `keywords` meta tag exists in Backend > SEO; it does nothing for Google. Not worth any time.
- **L5.** Bratislava's three English "Liga" pages and the SLTA city pages share the same description patterns; each city needs its own wording (venues, season names, a local sentence) so the city pages are not near-duplicates of each other.

## 7. Links and authority (off-page)

- Ask every sponsor, partner and venue on your court pages to link to the matching BLTA page (the sponsor strip and court pages already name them).
- Venue pages: ask each venue to link back; offer the "Zápasy odohrané na tomto kurte" block as a reason.
- Slovak tennis media and clubs (the Slovak Tennis Association's regional pages, club websites, local sport portals, city and university sport pages in Bratislava and Košice): send a short press note per season with results and a photo.
- Social profiles: use the same name and link back to the right page (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube are already in the footer; add them to the `sameAs` property).
- Embeds: the live-score and rankings embeds on blta.sk's partner pages can include a visible "powered by" link to the right page.
- Competitors exist (for example the Bratislava Amateur Tennis League run by TK Pro Set appears in a search for the main query). Look at which pages they rank with before choosing keywords; this audit did not analyse competitor pages.
