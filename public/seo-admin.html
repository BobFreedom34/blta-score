SEO

The address (slug) of every page and what Google and link previews (WhatsApp, Facebook…) show for it: the title, the description, the keywords and the preview picture — in Slovak and in English. The Slovak page lives at /slug, the English one at /en/slug, and the language switch jumps between the two. The texts of a season, player, court or bracket page take the name in {name}.

When you change an address the old one stops working at once — there is no redirect, so links, bookmarks and search results that still use it show “not found”.
